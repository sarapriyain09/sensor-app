Core Reliability Calculations

The metrics your engineers already use

MTBF.co.uk computes these for every tracked asset, automatically, from your maintenance history.

MTBF
Mean Time Between Failures
Total Uptime ÷ No. of Failures
How long equipment runs before the next breakdown.
MTTR
Mean Time To Repair
Total Repair Time ÷ No. of Repairs
How fast your team restores failed equipment to service.
Availability
Asset Availability %
MTBF ÷ (MTBF + MTTR) × 100
The percentage of time an asset is operational and ready.
Failure Rate
Failure Rate (λ)
λ = 1 ÷ MTBF
Frequency of failures per unit of operating time.
