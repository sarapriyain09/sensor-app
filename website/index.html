Core Reliability Metrics

The calculations that drive decisions

We compute these for every asset, automatically, from your maintenance logs.

MTBF
Mean Time Between Failures
Total Uptime ÷ No. of Failures
How long your equipment runs before breaking down.
MTTR
Mean Time To Repair
Total Repair Time ÷ No. of Repairs
How fast your team restores failed equipment.
Availability
Asset Availability %
MTBF ÷ (MTBF + MTTR) × 100
Percentage of time your asset is operational and ready.
Failure Rate
Failure Rate (λ)
λ = 1 ÷ MTBF
Frequency of failures per unit of operating time.
